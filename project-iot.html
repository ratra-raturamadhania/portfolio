01 — Overview

About the project.

This project explored the use of IoT technology for monitoring narrow or difficult-to-reach areas using a compact mobile robot.

The system used ESP32-CAM as the main controller and camera module, allowing users to monitor live video while remotely controlling the robot's movement.

The project gave me hands-on experience combining hardware, electronics, networking, and embedded programming into one working prototype.

02 — My Role

What I worked on.

01

Hardware Assembly

Assembled the ESP32-CAM, motor driver, DC motors, power components, and supporting electronic connections into the robot prototype.

02

ESP32-CAM Programming

Configured and programmed the ESP32-CAM using Arduino IDE to support camera streaming and network-based control.

03

Motor Control

Integrated the L298N motor driver with DC motors to control forward, backward, left, and right movement.

04

Troubleshooting

Diagnosed connection, power, camera, and control issues during prototype development and testing.

03 — How It Works

From camera to movement.

01

ESP32-CAM

Captures video from the robot and streams it through a local network connection.

02

Web Control

Users access the robot through a browser interface to view the camera stream and send movement commands.

03

L298N

Receives control signals from the ESP32-CAM and regulates the DC motors used for robot movement.
